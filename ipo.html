[05]how to use this page

no retail broker API exposes IPOs; data is maintained in data/ipo.json
Dates firstOpen and close are bidding days. Allotment usually lands one working day after close, refunds and demat credit the next, and listing the day after that.
Minimum bidOne lot at the top of the price band. Retail bids are in lots up to ₹2 lakh; above that you are in the non-institutional bucket.
Fields you can addsubscribed_x (times subscribed), listing_price and allotment date in the JSON show up here automatically when present.
