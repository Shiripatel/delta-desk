[03]how the council works

rules model v0
Narrow agentsEach agent looks at one thing: trend, momentum, MACD, volume, volatility, levels, valuation, growth, quality, ownership. It returns a vote from −1 to +1, a confidence, and the numbers it saw.
Horizon sets the weightsAt 15 minutes the technical agents carry 95 % of the vote. At 10 years the fundamental agents carry 85 %. Slide the horizon and watch the lines thicken and thin.
Verdict, not a black boxThe verdict is the weighted vote. Every input is in the table below it, so a strange call can always be traced to the agent that made it.
