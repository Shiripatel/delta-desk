not chatter,typed trades.

Six narrow agents read the NIFTY option chain, agree on a market regime, and emit one typed decision with a calibrated confidence. Below the approval threshold nothing happens. Above it, a human approves in paper mode, and the execution agent places the order in live mode.

Decisions, not stringsEvery output is a schema: side, instrument, lots, limit, stop, confidence.
Calibrated confidence0.81 means roughly 81 of 100 such calls worked in the backtest window.
Risk before executionMargin, max loss and Greek limits are checked by a separate agent that can veto.
